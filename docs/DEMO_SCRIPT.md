# Desktop walkthrough

1. Open the workspace without signing in to explore sample data. The sample banner identifies the records, and saving prompts account creation.
2. Click the notification bell to open recent handoffs. Dismiss with Escape, the close button or an outside click. Opening it does not scroll the page.
3. Open either profile menu, choose Appearance, and switch between light, dark and device settings. Reload to check persistence.
4. Load the OpenStreetMap view. Use its zoom controls or select a zone to focus its coordinates. Sample locations remain identified.
5. Create an email/password account. The account starts with no sample records. Email ownership remains unverified until verification mail or Google sign-in succeeds.
6. Create an event with optional coordinates, add a recovery zone, and add a compatible partner in Settings.
7. Capture a material, confirm weight provenance, assign its destination and record collection.
8. Upload recipient receipt evidence, enter accepted/rejected quantities and explain discrepancies. Explicitly review the receipt before verifying recovery. Exclude rejected material from recovered totals.
9. Issue a signed passport and inspect its public verification page. Signatures attest to the recorded data, not independently proven physical delivery.
10. Check profile settings and logout. Only configured Google OAuth and SMTP integrations are presented as available. No live email or Google authorization was exercised without credentials.

Use disposable accounts and records for review. No native phone application is included, and no GitHub publication should happen before the user's approval for this revision.
